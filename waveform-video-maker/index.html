Make a waveform or equalizer video

Milani can draw your voice or your music as a sound wave: a line, bars, a circle, dots, or an audio equalizer, then save that as a video. The drawing follows the sound you upload. It is made in the browser by code, not by an AI clip generator.

Open the waveform Open the equalizer

Two different waveforms

The editor shows a small waveform as soon as you record or upload. That one is a tool. Two handles on it choose the start and the end of the passage that will be in the video. It is not, by itself, the picture people will see.

The picture is a design you pick. In the template list, under “Or make your own”, the sound-wave designs are full picture, bars, circle, equalizer, orbit, line, waves, and dots. Pick one and the preview draws it across the frame, in time with playback.

The designs

Waves

A flowing sound wave. A clear first choice when you want the video to read as a waveform. Open waves.

Equalizer

The spectrum, drawn as an audio equalizer. Use it when the video should feel like a listening meter rather than a landscape. Open equalizer.

Bars

Columns that rise and fall with the sound. Simpler than the equalizer, and easy to read on a phone. Open bars.

Circle, orbit, line, dots

Circle and orbit wrap the sound. Line keeps a single spare trace. Dots scatter it. They are the same idea: the audio is the drawing. Open them from the template list in the editor.

Full picture

The frame fills with a photo or with one of Reza Milani’s generative artworks, instead of a wave. The sound still drives that picture. For several artworks cut on the beat, use the music visualizer.

How to make the video

  1. Open Voice video, then record or upload a voice or a song.
  2. Use the handles on the small waveform to keep the part you want in the video.
  3. Under Or make your own, pick waves, bars, equalizer, circle, orbit, line, dots, or full picture.
  4. Set the background, line colour, and accent. Add a title if you want one, and subtitles for speech.
  5. Press Make my video. Milani encodes the file on your device. A free export is the first 30 seconds.

Colour is part of the design. The Colour tab sets the background, the lines, and an accent, or you can surprise yourself with a palette and go back to the template colours. The frame can be a post, a square, a story, or wide. 1080p is the right size for X, Instagram, and TikTok.

A waveform video is a good fit for a voice note, a chorus, or a short podcast excerpt when you want the sound itself to be the image. If you also want the words on screen, subtitles run in the browser and you can edit each line. For a spoken episode with a photo and a title, the audio to video page and the Podcast template are the more direct path.

Your audio is not uploaded. The preview, the wave, and the file are made on your device. A free video includes a small Made with Milani mark. Longer, mark-free exports are for collectors of Reza Milani’s art on Verse.

Questions

Which waveform design should I open first?

Waves is a direct sound-wave picture. Equalizer is the spectrum as bars. Bars is a simpler column reading. Circle and orbit wrap the sound. Line is spare. Dots scatters it. Full picture fills the frame when you would rather show a photo or an artwork, with the sound still driving the page.

Is the waveform in the editor the one in the video?

No. The small waveform under your file is for trimming: two handles choose the passage. The waveform in the video is a separate design you pick in the template list, and it is drawn across the frame in time with the sound.

Can I use a song as well as a voice?

Yes. Upload either. A waveform video does not care whether the sound is speech or music. If you want several generative artworks cut on the beat, use Music video mode instead.

Where is the audio equalizer?

In the editor, open Voice video, then the template named equalizer. You can also open it from this page. Colours for that design are in the Colour tab: background, lines, and accent.
